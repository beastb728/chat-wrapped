Your group chat,
wrapped.

Who texts the most, who starts every conversation, who leaves everyone on read. Stat cards for your Status, plus a Who Said It? party game.

  • Nothing uploaded Your chat is read inside your browser and never leaves the device.
  • Works offline Load the page, turn on airplane mode, then pick your file.
  • No login No account, no phone number, no password. Ever.
  • Open source Every line is public, with no third-party code.

How to export a chat

Android
  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp
  2. Tap ⋮ → More → Export chat
  3. Choose Without media
  4. Save it to your files (or share it straight to this site if you installed it as an app), then choose it above
iPhone
  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp and tap the chat name at the top
  2. Scroll down → Export Chat → Without Media
  3. Choose Save to Files
  4. Come back here, tap Choose your WhatsApp export and pick the .zip. No need to unzip it
