Don't trust us? Good. Load this page, turn on airplane mode, then choose your file. It still works, because nothing needs the internet.

How to export on Android
  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp
  2. Tap ⋮ → More → Export chat
  3. Choose Without media
  4. Save it to your files (or share it straight to this site if you installed it as an app), then choose it above
How to export on iPhone
  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp and tap the chat name at the top
  2. Scroll down → Export Chat → Without Media
  3. Choose Save to Files
  4. Come back here, tap Choose your WhatsApp export and pick the .zip. No need to unzip it
