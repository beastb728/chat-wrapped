Your chat never leaves your device.

Not "we delete it after". Not "it's encrypted". It is never sent anywhere. Here's how, and how you can check it yourself.

What happens when you pick a file

  1. Your browser reads the file from your phone or computer.
  2. If it's a .zip, it's unpacked by your browser's built-in decompression. There's no upload and no server.
  3. The text is read and counted in a background thread on your device.
  4. The cards are drawn on your screen. When you close the tab or tap "Start over", it's gone.

How you can verify it

  • Airplane mode test: open the site, switch on airplane mode, then choose your chat. Everything still works.
  • The counter: the green badge at the bottom counts every network request the page makes after you open your chat. It stays at 0.
  • Content Security Policy: the page tells your browser to block all outgoing connections (connect-src 'none'). Even a bug in our code couldn't send your chat anywhere.
  • Open source: every line of code is public and has no third-party libraries. There's no build step, so the code you read is the code that runs.

What we never do

  • No accounts, no login, and we will never ask for a password or phone number.
  • No cookies, no trackers, no ads.
  • Share cards contain statistics only, never the text of your messages. You can also hide everyone's names.

Your friends' privacy

A chat export contains your friends' messages too. Because nothing is uploaded, nobody but you ever sees the raw chat. Before posting cards with names on them, consider asking the group, or use Hide names.

Analytics

This version has no analytics at all. If we add any, it'll be cookieless page-view counting that never sees your chat, and we'll list exactly what's counted here.
